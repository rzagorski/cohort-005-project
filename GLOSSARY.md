# Cadence

An online course platform where Students buy courses from Instructors, individually or as Seats bought by a team.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who takes courses on Cadence, as opposed to an Instructor who teaches them.
_Avoid_: Learner

### Teams

**Team Admin**:
The person who buys Seats for a team and hands out their Seat Links. Only the Team Admin can make a Refund Request for a purchase of Seats.
_Avoid_: Team Owner, Buyer

**Seat**:
One Student's access to one course, bought by a team and handed out to whoever claims it.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Licence

**Seat Link**:
The link a team shares so that a Student can claim a Seat.
_Avoid_: Coupon Code, Coupon Link, Redeem Link

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a Student has taken through its Seat Link. Once claimed, the Seat belongs to the team and the Student cannot refund it on their own. A single Claimed Seat makes the whole purchase of Seats non-refundable.
_Avoid_: Redeemed Coupon, Used Coupon

### Refunds

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase during which the Student can make a Refund Request. The whole of the fourteenth day counts.
_Avoid_: Cancel Window, Cancellation Window

**Refundable Purchase**:
A purchase the Student can still make a Refund Request for: inside its Refund Window, with under 2 hours watched and under half its lessons completed, and with no Claimed Seat on it. A purchase of Seats is refunded only as a whole, never Seat by Seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellable Purchase

**Refund Request**:
A Student's ask to get their money back for a purchase, which the Cadence team reviews by hand and then approves or declines.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Pending Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the Cadence team has not yet approved or declined. A purchase can have at most one at a time.
_Avoid_: Open Refund Request, Requested Refund, Pending Chargeback

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the Cadence team has turned down. No Refund is owed and the Student keeps access.
_Avoid_: Rejected Refund Request, Denied Refund Request

**Refund**:
The money owed back to the Student once a Refund Request is approved: always the full amount they paid for the purchase. There are no partial Refunds.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Partial Refund

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund whose money has actually been sent back to the Student. Issuing, not approval, is what turns the purchase into a Refunded Purchase.

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund that was approved but whose money could not be sent back. The Student still has access, because they have still paid.

**Refunded Purchase**:
A purchase whose Refund has been issued. The Student no longer has access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Cancelled Purchase
